What we do

Three disciplines.
One idea running through them.

01

Brand strategy

The thinking everything else stands on.

We research the business, the market and the people it serves, then define the purpose, positioning and story the brand will own.

  • Research and insight
  • Purpose and positioning
  • Brand story and messaging
  • Naming support
02

Branding

An identity people recognise before they read it.

We turn the strategy into a complete visual and verbal system that holds up everywhere the brand appears.

  • Logo and visual identity
  • Typography and colour
  • Tone of voice
  • Brand guidelines
03

Packaging design

Where the brand is held, opened and judged.

We design packaging as part of the brand system, from structure and materials to the moment someone opens it.

  • Structure and materials
  • Range and label systems
  • Print and finish
  • Unboxing experience

About

One habit:cutting.

Sharp Story is an independent branding and creative studio. We help ambitious startups and impact-driven businesses build brands that are clear in purpose, distinct in look and made to last.

A brand is a promise you can say in one sentence.

Most brands don’t struggle from having too little to say. They struggle from saying too much: a purpose, three taglines and a look borrowed from the category leader. Our work starts by taking things away.

We combine research, strategic thinking and purposeful creativity. We listen first, find the one idea a brand can own, then build the identity and packaging around it, so every detail points the same way.

We’re independent by choice. The people you meet are the people doing the work, and we take on a small number of projects so each one gets our full attention.

Who we work with

Ambitious startups

Founders building something new who want a brand as considered as the product, from the first day it meets a customer.

Impact-driven businesses

Companies doing good work that deserve to be noticed for it, without sounding like everyone else who claims the same values.

How we think
01

Research first

Good branding starts with what’s true about the business, the market and the people it serves. We find that before we design anything.

02

One idea

A brand that says one thing clearly will beat one that says everything. We find the idea worth owning and cut the rest.

03

Purposeful craft

Every colour, word and fold should earn its place. Restraint is harder than decoration, and it lasts longer.

04

Built to grow

We design systems, not one-offs, so the brand holds up from the first pack to the hundredth product.

The process

How a brand gets sharp

  1. Listen

    Workshops, interviews and research into the business, the category and the customer.

  2. Cut

    Purpose, positioning and the one idea the brand will own, agreed before design begins.

  3. Shape

    Identity and packaging designed together as one system, and tested where the brand will live.

  4. Launch

    Guidelines, files and support, so your team can keep the brand sharp without us.
